Dilution Principle: Diluting an acid with distilled water decreases $[H^+]$ ion concentration:
pH = -log₁₀[H⁺]
A 10-fold dilution reduces $[H^+]$ by 10×, causing pH to increase by +1 unit toward neutrality (7).

Laboratory Safety & Objectives

Safety Precautions:

  • Wear protective laboratory goggles when handling hydrochloric acid ($HCl$).
  • Handle boiling tubes gently to prevent accidental breakage or spills.
  • Never invert or violently shake boiling tubes; use gentle swirling action.
  • Always add acid to water or mix dilutions with calibrated pipettes.

Assessment Learning Goals:

Understand the mathematical relationship between solution volume, solute molarity, and pH scale shift. Practice accurate volumetric laboratory technique using pipettes, droppers, and glazed reaction tiles.
